# Redesign Brief

**Mode:** Redesign · Overhaul, per the user-approved plan. This is a new static HTML/CSS/JS preview and a ZIP deliverable, not an edit to the live website.

| Preserve | Improve | Remove |
|---|---|---|
| User-supplied logo, palette reference, original Lemmy covers, portrait, and About photos | Recompose page hierarchy around the personal story and clear service pathways | Production-site changes or deployment |
| Existing route slugs, factual source copy, and verified booking/product/policy destinations | Make the About page photographic, connected, and personal | Unsupported copy, generic/stock imagery, invented credentials/testimonials/prices/product claims |
| Therapy, RECLAIM™, and Coaching as distinct offerings | Stronger peach/terracotta integration and vivid Lemmy Lou collection experience | Fake booking, contact, mailing-list, shopping-cart, or checkout behavior |
| Mobile-first access and accessible content | Consistent responsive layout, clear keyboard focus, reduced motion | Copying unfinished legal notices as if approved |

**Highest-risk changes:** personal narrative and third-party legal/commercial destinations. Personal content will be drawn from the supplied copy only; policy pages in this preview will link to the verified current official pages instead of rewriting legal copy. Existing product and booking destinations will remain external links. **Fallback:** if a source fact is unclear, omit it and link to the current source rather than inventing a replacement.
